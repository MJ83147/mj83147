How to steward

What a steward is

Stewards run the Mystery Bounty tables. You host a poker table in Torn, seat the players assigned to your table, keep play moving, and record what happens so the public board stays accurate. Everything players see on the board comes from what stewards report here.

Your job, start to finish

  • Set your availability. Mark the hourly slots (Torn City Time) you can cover. Admins assign tables from these, so keep them current.
  • Host your assigned tables. When you agree to host a table, you'll find the list of players for that table on this page. Each table has a Copy Torn IDs button. Click it to copy the IDs to your clipboard, then paste them into the poker tournament settings. Make the table name exactly match the table name shown here so players can easily find the right table to join. Also set the start time and the max number of players correctly.
  • Keep track of what happens. Note who leaves and when. During a knockout it is important to record who knocked out who. You can record results here as they happen, which is better than waiting until the table finishes.
  • Message players. The Sample messages tab (in the box at the top of this page) has pre-written messages you can send to players on your table. For example, to tell a player they've advanced, who to send rebuy money to, or that they've been paid.

The stages

  • Day 16-max tables, bounties live. One winner advances to Day 2.
  • RebuySecond chance for eliminated players, no bounties. One winner advances.
  • Day 2Bounties live. Top 3 per table reach the Final.
  • Final TableBounties live. Plays to one champion.
  • Money TableSettles the remaining money places.

Reporting a result

  • Rebuy: tap the winner (the one player who advances), then Submit.
  • Day 1 / Day 2 / Final (bounty tables): for each player, set who knocked them out. Only the table winner is left blank; everyone else was knocked out by someone. Then Submit. This records every knockout and the finishing order at once. Position auto-fills from the bottom up as you set "knocked out by": the first player you mark out gets last place, the next gets second-from-last, and so on in the order you fill them in. So for two fast knockouts you just set "knocked out by" on both and they land in the right positions automatically. If the auto order is wrong (two busted at once and you entered them the wrong way, or you want a tie), type over the position. Anything you type by hand is kept and never overwritten by the auto-fill. Split knockout: if two players knock out the same player with equal hands, set "knocked out by" to one of them, then pick the other in the "split with" box that appears under it. The envelope is split equally between the two, and each is credited with the knockout.
  • Money Table: set the full finishing order, then Submit.
